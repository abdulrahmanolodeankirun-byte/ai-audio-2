Quick Context – Lagos

Lagos is a fast‑growing megacity facing challenges such as flooding, heat waves, poor air quality, and energy demand. Applying climate control strategies here is essential for resilience and sustainable development.

1) What is Climate Control?

Climate control in this report refers to actions aimed at reducing greenhouse gas emissions (mitigation) and adapting to climate impacts (adaptation). For example, renewable energy reduces emissions, while flood‑resilient housing reduces vulnerability.

2) Application in Society

Mitigation

  • Renewable energy (solar in homes, markets, and schools).
  • Efficient transport (BRT systems, electric vehicles).
  • Waste reduction and recycling.

Adaptation

  • Drainage upgrades and flood‑resilient housing in Lagos communities.
  • Heat‑tolerant crops for Nigerian agriculture.
  • Disaster preparedness and early‑warning systems.

3) Benefits of Climate Control

Environmental

Cleaner air and water, reduced flooding, better waste management.

Economic

New jobs in renewable energy, reduced healthcare costs, improved productivity.

Social

Better living conditions, improved resilience in low‑income areas, safer housing.

4) Lagos‑Focused Strategies

Flood Management

Constructing proper drainage, restoring wetlands in areas like Lekki and Ajah, and discouraging building on floodplains.

Energy Solutions

Encouraging rooftop solar in markets and schools, reducing dependence on diesel generators which worsen air quality.

Urban Greening

Planting trees along major roads (e.g., Ikorodu Road) to combat heat and improve air quality.

Transport

Expanding BRT and rail networks to reduce car use and cut emissions.
